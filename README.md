   RetailMax Group – Retail Business Analytics

An interactive Power BI project analysing retail business performance across four areas:

Executive Performance

Customer Profitability & Retention

Channel Performance

Product Pricing & Discount Strategies

Key findings: £75.24M revenue, £18.74M net profit and 24.91% profit margin.

Tools: Power BI, DAX, Power Query and Excel.

Objective: Transform retail data into actionable insights to improve profitability, customer retention, sales performance and pricing strategies.
